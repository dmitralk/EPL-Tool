# Getting started & your access

EPL Tool keeps Lubmarine's export price lists in one database: customers, products, the Standard EPL, packaging charges
and every price list sent. You create price lists with a wizard, export them to Excel in the standard format, and open
email drafts with the file attached.

## How EPL Tool knows who you are

There is **no password**. When the app starts it reads your **Windows account** and your **company email** from
Windows, and looks you up in the user list stored in the database. The user list is managed by the admins in
**Settings → Users**.

- If you are not on the list (or your access was deactivated) you see a **No access** screen with the admins' names
  and a **Request access** button, which opens an email draft to them with your details.
- Your **role** (Admin, User or Viewer) decides what you can change: see [Roles & permissions](help:roles).
- For each customer, its **pricing management mode** decides who may change it: see
  [Pricing management modes](help:pricing-modes).

Your name and role are shown at the bottom of the sidebar. The box at the top of this **Guides & Help** page shows your
access, the customers you manage and the admins to contact.

## The main screens

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Totals and the most recent price lists |
| Price Lists | All price lists; export, email, compare, delete |
| Customers | Customer master data, contacts, pricing management, history |
| Emails | Every customer / internal email address and the customers that use it |
| Analysis | Prices of all customers next to the Standard EPL |
| Products, Standard EPL | The product catalogue and the versioned standard prices |
| Settings | Database, users, activity log, backups, logo, PBP emails, units, packaging, currencies |

## Two things to remember

- **Exports and email drafts are always allowed**, for every role and every customer: they only read data.
- Every change is recorded in the [activity log](help:activity), which everybody can read.
