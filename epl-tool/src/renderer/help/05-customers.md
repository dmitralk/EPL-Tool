# Customers & New Customer

The **Customers** screen lists all active customers with their currency and pricing management mode. Use the customer
filter or **My customers** (the customers you manage).

## The customer page

- **Details** and **Contacts** — click **Edit** to change master data and the *To customer* / *Internal copy*
  addresses. *Edit* is shown only if you may change the customer; otherwise a lock shows why.
- The **PBP copy / PBP common** addresses come from Settings → PBP Emails and are the same for all customers.
- **Pricing management** — the mode and managers; see [Pricing management modes](help:pricing-modes).
- **Price Lists** — tick two to compare them side by side.
- **History** — this customer's entries from the [activity log](help:activity).

## New Customer

Admins and users click **New Customer** on the Customers screen.

- **Required:** Customer Ref SAP (must be unique — hidden customers count too), short name, full name, currency and
  packaging version.
- Optional: supply region, country, zone, customer type and its SAP ref, business model, SPOC, customer and internal
  emails.
- The PBP copy / common addresses are filled automatically from Settings → PBP Emails.
- Who manages the new customer: you, if you are a user; an admin chooses the mode.

Currencies and packaging versions must exist first (Settings → Currencies / Packaging, managed by admins).
