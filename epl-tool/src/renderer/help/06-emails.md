# Emails tab & PBP emails

## Customer and internal addresses

The **Emails** screen shows every address found in the customers' *To customer* and *Internal copy* fields, with the
customers that use it. Everybody can see all addresses.

- **Edit** (pencil) — tick or untick customers for one address. Customers you may not change are shown locked.
- **Replace** — swaps an address for another one in every customer **you may change** (merging duplicates).
- **Remove** — removes an address from every customer **you may change**.

When some customers using an address are managed by others, the dialog says so, e.g. *"4 of 7 customers — 3 are
managed by others and stay unchanged"*, and the result reports how many were skipped.

## PBP emails

**PBP Emails** (Settings) are the shared PBP inboxes, e.g. *PBP Costing* and *PBP Common*. Admins add, edit and remove
them.

- The entry whose name contains *Common* is the contact line at the bottom of exported price lists.
- *Costing* and *Common* fill the PBP copy / PBP common addresses of **new** customers.

## Email drafts

Every **Email** button opens the same dialog: edit the subject and body (placeholders `{customer}`,
`{customer_full}`, `{version}`, `{effective}`), choose a folder, and one Outlook draft per price list opens with the
Excel file attached. Recipients: the customer's To, Internal, PBP copy and PBP common addresses.

Your subject and body are saved as **your own template on this PC** when you send — not in the shared database. Until
your first send, a default text is used. Email drafts are allowed for every role.
